# A treasurer, three agents, and one room

Draft for Nigel to edit and publish under his own name if he wants the optional blog track. Not submitted.

University clubs still reimburse people by dropping a photo in chat and hoping the treasurer copies it into a spreadsheet. Treasurer Desk puts that queue in one Switch room.

`@intake` turns the sentence into a claim. The amount and the date come from code. `@auditor` applies a policy document the treasurer can read: a cap, no alcohol, a receipt window, a per-event budget. The verdict is `PASS`, `FLAG`, or `BLOCK`. A model, if one is configured, may only phrase the paragraph. `@bookkeeper` writes the treasurer's decision onto a shared ledger and prints the e-transfer line. It does not send the money.

Sam can ask for the ledger. Sam cannot approve. That refusal is a message in the room, next to the claim.

The demo on this repo was recorded in rules mode, with no model key. The replies in `docs/demo.mp4` were produced by the running agents. The people are fictional.
